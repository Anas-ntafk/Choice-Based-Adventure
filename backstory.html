The Backstory

Henry was born in a small village on the outskirts of the kingdom. When mercenaries attacked his home, he lost his family and everything he knew. A royal commander sent to deal with the attackers found the young boy among the survivors and took him back to the palace, raising him as his own.

The commander who was responsible for training the two princes. Henry grew up alongside the Crown Guards, eventually earning his place among them and being assigned as the personal guard of the younger prince, who had become his closest friend.

Years later, the kingdom faces an uncertain future. The king is bedridden, and the Crown Prince, heir to the throne, has mysteriously disappeared. The king's brother, who never considered his nephew fit to rule, assumes he has simply fled his responsibilities. Unwilling to abandon his brother, the younger prince entrusts Henry with a mission: find the missing Crown Prince and bring him home. Henry sets out, unaware of the events that await him.
